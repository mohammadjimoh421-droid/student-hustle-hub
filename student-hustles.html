🚀 How to Start Your First Student Hustle

1. Choose One Skill

Don't try to learn everything at once. Choose one skill that interests you and focus on it.

2. Learn for Free

Use the free learning resources on this page and practice regularly.

3. Practice Your Skill

Create 3–5 examples of your work so people can see what you can do.

4. Show Your Work

Share your work with friends, businesses and people who may need your services.

5. Get Your First Client

Start small, deliver good work and build trust. Your first goal can be earning your first legitimate ₦1,000.

🚀 Start Your Hustle

Learn a skill, practice it, build examples of your work and start looking for genuine clients. Your first goal isn't to get rich—it is to build a useful skill and earn your first legitimate ₦1,000.
